The real problem

Dinner becomes a job because someone has to remember everything.

What can we eat that everyone will actually finish? That isn't what we had two days ago? That we have the ingredients for? That doesn't take an hour? That the kids won't pick apart?

That someone is probably you. And you're exhausted by it.

This is the problem VetoChef solves

Not "what's for dinner." The mental load before dinner.

The hard part is holding every family member's preferences in your head, every single day, and producing an answer that works for all of them before 6pm.

VetoChef holds it instead. You tell it once what doesn't work: an ingredient, a cuisine, a dish that went badly last time. It remembers. You never have to factor it in again.

When it suggests something and misses, one tap and a single word is all it needs. It adjusts. You don't manage it, you just correct it occasionally and it gets better.
