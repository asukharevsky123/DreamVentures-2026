How it works

Every veto gets remembered.

Someone in your house hates mushrooms. Someone else won't touch spicy food. Your teenager has decided this week they're pescatarian. Your partner just wants pizza on Fridays.

VetoChef holds all of that. Every veto your family casts gets remembered permanently: no re-explaining, no overrides. The AI only ever suggests meals that clear every single person in your household.

And when it gets something wrong? One tap. Gone. It asks why, in a sentence. Then it never makes that mistake again.
